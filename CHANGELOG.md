# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-10)

First public release.

- A hex gem puzzle for WoW: Forever that opens as your flight takes off. Turn gems three at a time to match colors.
- Starflowers, black pearls, starred bonus gems and bombs across seven levels. Touching black pearls win the run.
- One run per account, kept after every move and carried across flights and characters.
- A settings page under **Options > AddOns > Skyhex**: open on takeoff, hide on landing, modern or classic gems, sound and window size.
- Practice boards with `/skyhex try` show each special move without touching your run.
